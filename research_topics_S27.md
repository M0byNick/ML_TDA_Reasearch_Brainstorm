## 5. Recommendation and sequencing

1. **C1 (topology-aware grid forecasting)** is the best single bet: it is co-advisable (Evans for grid and forecasting, Giunti for vineyards/stability), uses every course, sits in a market with the strongest hiring tailwind (utility capex, data-center load), and public data exists (NYISO, PJM, EIA-930).
2. **C7 (layout optimization with topological constraints)** is the most direct match to Evans's stated focus and to Giunti's multiparameter work, with a fresh literature base (MP-Grid 2026). Higher technical risk; strong thesis-level novelty.
3. **C3 (delivery-network topology)** is the cheapest to start because your 592 code and the Olist data already exist, and it gives Giunti a concrete canopies/vineyards application. Good as a first paper or a warm-up for C1.
4. **C4 (PH-enhanced LLM evaluation)** is the high-upside wildcard: it extends Giunti's newest preprint, reuses your legislative LLM pipeline, and targets the fastest-growing software market in the list.

A practical path: start C3 in Fall 2026 as the "TDA + ML on data you already own" warm-up, pitch C1/C7 to both professors as the S27 project, and keep C4 as a side paper.
